Privacy Policy

Your data, handled with the same care as our barrel-aged stouts. Here's what we collect and why.

On This Page

  • 1. Data We Collect
  • 2. How We Use Data
  • 3. Cookies & Local Storage
  • 4. Sharing & Retention
  • 5. Your Rights & Choices
  • 6. Security & Children
  • 7. Contact & Updates

Related: Terms of Service · Sitemap

1. Data We Collect

Directly from you: name, email, phone, reservation details (party size, date), account credentials, age-verification confirmation (21+), newsletter preferences, and messages sent via contact / enquiry forms or auth forms.

Automatically: device/browser type, pages visited, and theme/direction preferences stored in your browser's localStorage (liquid_theme, liquid_dir). We do not use third-party tracking pixels on this static demo site.

2. How We Use Data

We use your data to confirm reservations and event bookings, manage Liquid Club accounts, send tap-release newsletters you've opted into, enforce our 21+ policy and Terms, improve taproom operations, and comply with alcohol-service and tax record-keeping laws.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use strictly-necessary browser storage only: theme (dark/light) and text direction (ltr/rtl) so the site remembers your display choices. No advertising cookies are set. Newsletter and enquiry forms process data in-browser for this demo and show a confirmation modal — connect a real backend/ESP before collecting production emails.

4. Sharing & Retention

We never sell personal data. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the taproom (e.g., reservation tools, email delivery, payment processors), or when required by law. Reservation records are kept up to 3 years for tax/compliance; marketing emails are kept until you unsubscribe via any newsletter email or by contacting us.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data, and may opt out of marketing at any time. To exercise rights, email hello@liquidneon.brew with subject "Privacy Request". We respond within 30 days. California / EU visitors: you retain CCPA/GDPR-equivalent rights including non-discrimination for exercising them.

6. Security & Children

We apply reasonable safeguards (TLS in production, least-privilege access, staff training). No system is 100% secure — please don't send sensitive payment data via plain contact forms. This site is not directed at children under 13, and alcohol content is intended for adults 21+ only.

7. Contact & Updates

Controller: Liquid Neon Craft Brewery & Taproom — via contact page or hello@liquidneon.brew. If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and note changes on the brewery blog. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.
