Est. 2018 • Independent Brewery

Forged In Hop Dust & Local Pride

What started as a tiny garage homebrew setup with a 10-gallon kettle has grown into a cornerstone craft institution. Liquid Neon was born out of a desire to create authentic, unpasteurized beers for our neighborhood.

We transformed a decommissioned 1920s brick warehouse in the historic industrial district into a warm, buzzing sanctuary for beer enthusiasts and families alike.

Guiding Principles

Our Craft Philosophy

We reject corporate shortcuts, extract additives, and forced pasteurization.

Quality Over Quantity

We restrict our annual production capacity to preserve strict quality control over every batch that leaves our fermentation tanks.

Relentless Experimentation

We embrace bold yeast strain hybridizations, unusual fruit mashes, and exotic barrel wood aging profiles.

Freshness Guaranteed

Our beer is kept cold from the conditioning cellar straight to your glass. No shelf-stale bottles, ever.

Uncompromising Craftsmanship

From Grain to Glass

Every step of our production workflow is handled in-house under the watchful eye of our head brewer.

01

1. Malt Sourcing
