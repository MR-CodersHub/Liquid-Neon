Terms of Service

The house rules for enjoying Liquid Neon — in the taproom and on this website. Please drink responsibly.

Questions? Contact us or see our Privacy Policy.

1. 21+ Alcohol Policy

You must be 21 years or older to purchase or consume alcohol at Liquid Neon, or to create an account that enables alcohol purchases. We check valid government photo ID for all alcohol service. We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone who is visibly intoxicated, cannot provide valid ID, or violates responsible-service laws. Never drink and drive — please use a rideshare or designated driver.

2. Website & Account Use

Accounts created via Login / Register must use accurate information. You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential. You agree not to misuse the site, attempt unauthorized access, scrape content, submit false reservations, or post unlawful, abusive or infringing material through forms, reviews or newsletter signups.

3. Orders, Bookings & Events

Tasting flights, tours, private buyouts and memberships listed on Pricing & Packages are subject to availability. Reservations made via Contact / Enquiry are confirmed only after staff reply. Cancellations with 48+ hours notice receive a full refund or rebooking; late cancellations may forfeit deposits. Gift cards are non-refundable, non-redeemable for cash except where required by law, and expire 5 years from purchase.

4. Taproom Conduct

Our taproom is a community space. Harassment, discrimination, vandalism, outside alcohol, or smoking/vaping indoors is prohibited. Children and dogs (leashed, patio only) are welcome when accompanied by an adult. Management may remove guests who endanger staff or visitors, with no refund.

5. Intellectual Property

All site content — logos, "Liquid Neon" branding, beer names, photography, copy and the maintenance vector illustration — is owned by Liquid Neon Craft Brewery & Taproom or its licensors. You may share links and personal photos with credit, but may not copy, resell or create derivative commercial works without written permission.

6. Liability & Disclaimers

This website and taproom information (hours, tap lists, allergens) are provided "as is" and may change without notice — see Maintenance or Coming Soon during updates. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Liquid Neon is not liable for indirect or consequential damages arising from site use, event attendance, or consumption. Allergen notice: our kitchen and brewery handle gluten, nuts, dairy and sulfites.

7. Changes & Contact

We may update these terms; material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. For questions, enforcement or DMCA requests: Liquid Neon Craft Brewery & Taproom, via contact page or hello@liquidneon.brew.
