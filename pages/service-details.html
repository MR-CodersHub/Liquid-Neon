Step inside our 4,000 sq ft industrial craft hall where 16 cold-conditioned craft taps flow directly from our stainless fermenters located right behind glass walls.

Visitor Guide

What To Expect When You Visit

Everything designed to deliver a comfortable, social, and memorable craft beer outing.

Seating Options

Choose from hand-carved oak long tables for big groups, intimate high-top bar seats, or cozy leather lounge armchairs.

Heated Dog Patio

Our outdoor beer garden is 100% dog-friendly, equipped with overhead radiant heaters, fire pits, and fresh water bowls.

Artisan Food Trucks

Every Thursday through Sunday we host rotating gourmet food trucks serving wood-fired pizza, smoked smashburgers, and tacos.

Interactive Flight Paddles

Custom Flight Builder

Not sure where to start? Build your own 4-pour flight paddle ($14) or ask our taproom staff to curate a progression from light sour to heavy imperial stout.

Flight #1: Hop Explorer

Pale Ale, West Coast IPA, Hazy IPA, Double IPA.

Flight #2: Dark & Barrel

Porter, Espresso Stout, Rye Cask, Imperial Stout.

Design & Vibe

Industrial Meets Modern Warmth

Exposed brickwork, warm ambient Edison bulb illumination, custom copper metal accents, and glass viewports into the brewhouse.

Warm Lighting

Subtle amber glow that makes every pint photo pop.

Acoustic Balance

Sound-dampening wooden ceiling baffles for effortless conversation.

Brewery View

Full glass wall into active fermentation tanks.

Games & Chill

Classic shuffleboard table and curated board game shelf.
