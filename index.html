Craft & Obsession

Small-Batch Philosophy. Uncompromised Quality.

Founded in 2018 by local master brewers, Liquid Neon was built on a simple conviction: beer tastes best when brewed without shortcuts. We source grain directly from independent family farms and ferment in open-top copper kettles.

🌾 Pure Grain

100% Floor-malted Maris Otter barley & local wheat varieties.

🍃 Whole Hop Cones

Freshly harvested Washington Yakima Valley whole-cone hops.
